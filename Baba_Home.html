Questions

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no watermarks and no accounts.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Editing and export happen entirely in your browser.

Which browser works best?

A recent version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer.

How long does a video export take?

It records in real time, so it takes about as long as the video. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
