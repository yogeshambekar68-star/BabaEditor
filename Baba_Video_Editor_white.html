Help

  1. Press Import video and choose a clip.
  2. Use the tabs on the left (Anim, Text, Effects, Filters…) and tap any option to preview it.
  3. Click the timeline to move the playhead; click a clip to select it.
  4. Drag text on the video to position it.
  5. Press Export video and keep the tab open until it finishes.

Shortcuts: Space play/pause · ← → step · S split · Esc close this page
